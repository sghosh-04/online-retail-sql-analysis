# Enterprise Financial Inclusion Analytics Platform

## Objective

Build an interactive Business Intelligence platform that helps policymakers,
economists, and financial institutions analyze global financial inclusion
using World Bank Global Findex data.

---

## Business Questions

### Financial Inclusion

1. Which countries have the highest account ownership?
2. Which countries have the lowest account ownership?
3. How has financial inclusion changed over time?

### Regional Analysis

4. Which region has the highest financial inclusion?
5. Which region improved the most?

### Income Group Analysis

6. Does income group influence account ownership?
7. How does digital finance vary across income groups?

### Digital Finance

8. Which countries lead in mobile money adoption?
9. Which regions have the highest digital payment usage?

### Borrowing & Savings

10. Which countries rely most on borrowing?
11. How do savings behaviors differ across regions?

### Financial Exclusion

12. What are the biggest barriers to opening a bank account?
13. Are physical distance, cost, or documentation the main obstacles?

### Trend Analysis

14. Which countries improved the fastest between survey years?
15. Which indicators improved globally over time?

# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Account Ownership Rate

## Digital Finance

- Digital Account Usage

## Savings

## Borrowing
